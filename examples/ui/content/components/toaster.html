Toaster

{{ .Params.description }}

A toast reaches the Toaster three ways: a <Toast> rendered anywhere (including one inserted later by an action), brick.emit("toast", {title, description, variant, duration, id}), or toast() imported from "brick:Toaster" in a client module. Only the first Toaster on a page is active, so a page may carry its own even when the layout has one.
